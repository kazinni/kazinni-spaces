How WorkFit works

From club to community

WorkFit runs a network of community fitness clubs — each with its own trainers, weekly schedule, member base, equipment and revenue. Members pay a monthly subscription, attend sessions, borrow books from the club library, and buy training merchandise from the WorkFit shop.

1
A club is registered — location, capacity, trainers, and weekly sessions.
2
Members join at Basic, Standard, or Premium tier — each with different access.
3
Attendance is tracked per session per week, giving each member a compounding streak.
4
The club library lends books and blogs to members — with borrowing tracked and overdue alerts.
5
Merch and equipment are sold through the shop, with orders and revenue tracked separately.

How BrandInn works

From brief to brand

BrandInn is a full-service branding studio built for Kenyan SMEs. Every client gets a public brand page, an active project pipeline, and access to print and design production — all tracked in one place.

1
A client signs up and picks a plan — Basic, Standard or Complete Branding.
2
A project is created with milestones, a service type and target date.
3
Work is dispatched to a tasker or Kasikazi board — design, print or content.
4
Deliverables ship — logos, banners, business cards, websites or social content.

How It Works

A simple, email-driven workflow designed to keep you focused on the work.

Step 01

Receive Invite

A KazInni admin emails you a FlexiKazi invite with your login credentials.

Step 02

Sign In & Review

Sign in to see tasks assigned to you. Each task arrives with a full brief by email.

Step 03

Complete & Submit

Accept the task on your board. Complete it and submit proof through the system.

Step 04

Get Paid

Once approved, payment is sent to your M-Pesa within 48 hours.

How Fusion Foods works

From market to kitchen

Fusion Foods is a B2B produce supplier built for Nairobi's restaurant and grocery economy. Orders flow through three stages — placed → confirmed → delivered — and each order is priced against a weekly price list that is kept in sync with the market.

1
Restaurants place orders via the Fusion Foods portal, WhatsApp, or the operator at the shop.
2
Orders are picked and packed from the supply store (Gitanga Rd, Naivasha Rd, or Dagoretti).
3
A Pick Up rider delivers the order and the customer confirms receipt in the portal.
4
Payment is settled via M-Pesa, cash on delivery, or weekly invoicing.

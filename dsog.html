How DSOG Fashion works

From design to doorstep

DSOG Fashion is a franchise-first apparel brand. Every product is designed in-house, printed locally, and shipped to franchise stores — who then retail to customers across the country.

1
Products are designed and added to the catalogue with images, colours, sizes, fabric and stock.
2
Franchisees place orders through the DSOG Franchise Portal, either for retail stock or customer-specific orders.
3
Orders are dispatched via Pick Up riders — same-day for Nairobi, next-day for other regions.
4
Customers receive their order, confirm delivery in the portal, and the franchise settles with HQ.

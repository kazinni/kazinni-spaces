How E-Huduma works

From request to delivery

E-Huduma is a transactional service bureau. Each request becomes an application with a fixed fee, an assigned staff member, and — when needed — a linked print job that handles the physical documents.

1
Client requests a service — walk-in, phone, or WhatsApp.
2
Staff accepts and assigns the application, records the payment (M-Pesa reference or cash).
3
Portal interaction — KRA iTax, eCitizen, NTSA, etc. Staff completes the online portion.
4
Documents are printed, scanned or copied — tracked in the print queue with a file link.
5
Delivered to client and application marked completed.
